## What this business actually is

Maspes Piante e Fiori is a historic family-run florist in Como, Italy, operating since 1954 from a brick-and-mortar shop at Via Leone Leoni 2. They sell cut flowers (especially roses, sold in classic quantity tiers — 6, 9, 12, 24, up to 100 stems), bouquets, plants, and floral compositions, and offer home delivery in Como and the surrounding province. Revenue comes from in-shop purchases, phone orders, and a Shopify-powered e-commerce storefront, with a "Pagamento Personalizzato" option that suggests they also accept custom-quoted orders (likely events, weddings, funerals, or large arrangements) [verify]. The legal entity is a partnership ("S.N.C.") run by the Maspes family — this is a traditional neighborhood fiorista, not a flower marketplace or subscription startup.

## Who the audience really is

- **Primary**: Como locals (roughly 30–65, often gifting) who need flowers for a specific occasion — anniversary, birthday, condolences, Valentine's, Mother's Day — and want a real florist with a phone number and a shop they can walk into, not a generic national delivery site. They're price-comfortable (€42–€700 for roses signals they're not the cheapest option) and care that the arrangement looks good when it arrives.
- **Secondary**: out-of-town senders ordering flowers *to* someone in Como (relatives, business contacts, expats sending home); event/wedding planners and hotels in the Lake Como area looking for a reliable local supplier; possibly journalists or local-business curators given the 70+ year history.

## What they're trying to convey

- **Established, trustworthy local institution** — "dal 1954," real address, real landline (031 27 11 36), full opening hours including Sunday morning. This is a multi-generational shop, not a dropship.
- **Quality and craft over commodity** — testimonials emphasize "rara bellezza," "professionalità," "composizioni raffinate"; roses priced at €7/stem position them above supermarket flowers.
- **Unintentionally: a small business that hasn't updated its web presence in a while** — sparse copy, generic Shopify theme cues, a footer credit "Realizzato da M.M.M." and a 2021-era favicon timestamp suggest the site hasn't kept pace with the in-shop quality the testimonials describe.

## What's broken about the current presentation

- **The hero says nothing.** "Benvenuti — Maspes Piante e Fiori" is a sign on a door, not a value proposition. The 70-year heritage, Como delivery, and craft positioning are all hidden in the page title and meta description instead of being the first thing a visitor sees.
- **No clear primary CTA.** A visitor arriving to buy flowers for tomorrow has no obvious "Order now / Call us / Same-day delivery" path — they get a product grid and have to figure it out.
- **Trust signals exist but are buried.** "Dal 1954" appears only in the SEO title; three strong testimonials are present but unattributed to a date or platform (Google? Facebook?) and sit below the product grid where most users won't scroll.
- **Catalog feels thin and rose-heavy.** The visible products are six rose SKUs plus one bouquet — for a real florist with plants, orchids, and compositions, this under-sells the range and makes the site read like a single-occasion gift shop.
- **Delivery promise is invisible.** Home delivery in Como and province is the single biggest reason someone picks a local florist over Interflora — and it's nowhere on the homepage body, only in the meta description.
- **Contact friction.** The phone number, the most likely conversion path for high-value orders (weddings, funerals, big arrangements), is in the footer rather than the header.

## What the rebuild should optimize for

**Primary**: Convert occasion-driven visitors (gifters needing flowers in Como within 24–48 hours) into either an online order or a phone call, by making delivery, heritage, and a clear "order now" path the spine of the homepage.
- *Secondary 1*: Capture high-value custom inquiries (weddings, events, funerals, large compositions) via a prominent, low-friction contact route tied to "Pagamento Personalizzato."
- *Secondary 2*: Reinforce the "fioraio storico dal 1954" positioning so Maspes wins on trust against generic national delivery aggregators ranking for "fioraio Como."
