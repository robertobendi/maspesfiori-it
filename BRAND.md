## Verdict

Full-rebrand. The analysis shows a 70-year craft florist whose in-shop quality (testimonials, heritage, bespoke compositions, daily delivery across Como province) is completely absent from a generic, sparse Shopify presentation with no value proposition, no clear CTA, buried trust signals, and a stale visual identity — the brand layer needs to be rebuilt from scratch so the site can finally match the shop.

## Palette

The colors in `source/palette.json` are payment-icon hex values (Visa, Amex, Mastercard, PayPal blues and reds) scraped from the footer, not brand colors — so the palette below is designed fresh around the heritage-fiorista positioning.

- `#2E5339` — Bosco — primary
- `#B25A5A` — Rosa Antica — accent
- `#F6F1E7` — Calce — surface
- `#1F1A17` — Inchiostro — ink
- `#A39A8B` — Salvia — muted

## Typography

`Heading: Fraunces` + `Body: Inter` — Fraunces brings the warm, slightly humanist serif character a multi-generational Italian florist deserves without tipping into stiff luxury, while Inter keeps product cards, prices, hours, and the "order now" path crisp and accessible for a 30–65 occasion-driven buyer scanning on a phone.

## Voice

Warm, considered, rooted. Writes in plain, confident Italian like a third-generation fiorista who knows that a 9-rose anniversary bouquet and a funeral arrangement are not the same conversation — never salesy, never corporate, always centered on the recipient, the occasion, and what arrives at the door.
